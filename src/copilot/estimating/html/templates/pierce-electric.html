The Pierce Electric Difference

Every project includes our quality assurance process:

  • Professional installation by licensed, background-checked electricians.
  • Premium materials from trusted manufacturers.
  • All electrical terminations torqued to manufacturer specifications.
  • Circuits clearly identified and labeled for future servicing.
  • Functional testing of the completed installation.
  • Jobsite cleaned upon completion.
  • A final walkthrough with the homeowner to review the completed work, answer questions, and ensure complete satisfaction before the project is considered complete.

Warranty

Pierce Electric stands behind the quality of our workmanship.

  • Lifetime Warranty on Labor
  • Manufacturer's Warranty on all materials and equipment furnished by Pierce Electric

Should a workmanship-related issue arise, we will return to correct it at no additional labor cost.

Exclusions

  • No costs are included for engineering, design, construction drawings, permits, permit fees, or inspections unless specifically identified.
  • No costs are included for upgrades or repairs to existing branch circuit wiring unless specifically identified.
  • No costs are included for concealed conditions or additional repairs discovered after work begins.
  • No costs are included for blocking, framing, drywall repair, patching, texture, or painting.
  • No costs are included for relocation of owner's furniture or personal property required to access the work area.
  • No costs are included for overtime or shift labor. All work will be performed during normal working hours.

Note: Visa and Master cards are acceptable for payment, 3.5% service fee added for processing.
